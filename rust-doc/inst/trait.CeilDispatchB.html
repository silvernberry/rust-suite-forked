Expand description

Dispatches ceil resolution from the most significant remaining counter dimension B.

B is the highest-order dimension considered after all higher dimensions have reached zero, and is the highest-order dimension of a two-counter tuple (A, B).

When B is non-zero, the default implementation resolves the ceil value by setting B to zero while preserving the lower dimension A.

When B is zero, the zero-state implementation delegates to CeilDispatchA, making A the final dimension available for ceil resolution.

This establishes the recursive progression from higher-significance dimensions toward the least significant dimension.

(A, B)
   B   current most significant dimension
   A
